# who/ — Actors

Orientation for agents: this directory holds the people, teams, and organisations involved.

## Actors in this campaign

- **Ally** (ally@lilypad.tech) — owner, product lead, first user. AuDHD; works in bursts,
  wants structure-before-content, small reviews, visuals, and self-enforcing systems.
- **Gamers Lab** — the POC client (ground-zero). A real business used to prove the pipeline.
  Its value props, website, and socials are the first context inputs to the system.
- **Future white-label clients** — businesses in Ally's network who have signalled this is a
  need. Each becomes a tenant in the productised version.
- **The end "lead"** — the customer/user/investor each client is trying to find. The whole
  pipeline exists to discover, qualify, and reach this actor.

## Roles in the product (RBAC, future)

- **Platform owner** (Ally) — manages tenants, billing, templates.
- **Client admin** — configures their business context, requirements, reviews AI output.
- **Client member** — runs campaigns, views leads.
